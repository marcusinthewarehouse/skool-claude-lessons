OpenClaw is a personal AI assistant that runs on your own machine and connects to the apps you already use - Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, and more.

**The three layers:**

- **Channels** - The messaging apps you already use. You talk to your assistant wherever you are.
- **Gateway** - The brain that runs on your machine. It receives messages, applies your rules, and decides what to do.
- **AI Model** - Usually Claude. The gateway sends your messages to the model and triggers tools on your behalf.

OpenClaw isn't an app you open - it's infrastructure that runs in the background 24/7. Message it from your phone at the gym, from your laptop at home, or from anywhere. It handles email, calendar, tasks, code, research, and more.

The mindset shift: you go from doing tasks yourself to delegating them to your assistant. You're the CEO, the assistant is your staff.
