Running OpenClaw on your daily driver laptop is risky - your personal accounts are exposed, your agent can mess up your work machine, and your laptop isn't always on.

**The fix:** Run OpenClaw on a dedicated machine that does nothing else.

**Your options:**

- **Old laptop** - Free if you have one. Just keep it plugged in and awake.
- **Mac Mini** - Best if you have the money to spare. Runs 24/7, quiet, $300-600.
- **Linux VPS** - $5-20/month, always on, no hardware to manage.

A dedicated machine creates a clean trust boundary - even if something goes wrong, the blast radius is limited to that one device. Create a fresh user account, don't sign into personal stuff, and your agent can only access what you explicitly allow.

You still control everything from your phone or main computer. The agent runs on the dedicated device, but you message it from anywhere.
