Caved and paid the $99 for windows 11 pro. The telegram harnesses and open source projects are for sure useful but I was hitting their limits more often than not. On pro you can download the windows app and the cloudflare one app and remote in to your direct PC screen and use as you would if you were sitting at the computer but from your phone. If you have a very specific and regimented workflow that is capable of being managed with the more common harnesses, more power to you and id love to compare notes. For building/testing as I would while sitting at the computer, this is by far my favorite. Worth the money in my opinion as I unlocked every aspect of my work now from my phone. Idk I got tired of hitting a bug mid day and having to wait until I’m home to fully flush it out because it was somewhere beyond Claude’s capabilities/access levels at the time.
