What If Your SOC2 Audit Was a Game?
Nobody wakes up excited about compliance. We're trying to change that — and we want you to build it with us.
Let's Be Honest
SOC2 sucks. It's the thing you dread, defer, and eventually panic about three weeks before an enterprise prospect asks for your trust report. You scramble through spreadsheets, screenshot your AWS console, pray your access reviews are "close enough," and hand an auditor a pile of PDFs that took your team two miserable weeks to assemble.
And gamification in enterprise software? Usually sucks too. Pointless badges. Confetti animations nobody asked for. A leaderboard that makes your top performer feel surveilled and everyone else feel inadequate.
What if you could take the most soul-crushing part of building enterprise software and make it the thing that actually drives user engagement?
That's what we're trying to build. And we want to do it live, with Claude Code, with anyone who's curious enough to try.
The Dumb Simple Idea
We're building an APM platform \(Application Portfolio Management\) on Supabase. Like most SaaS startups targeting enterprise and government, we need SOC2 Type II certification. So we did the responsible thing — we put audit triggers on our database tables. Every insert, update, delete gets logged. Who did it, what changed, when, full context.
Standard compliance stuff. Boring. Necessary.
Then someone asked: "Wait — if we're already logging every meaningful action a user takes... why can't we just read that same log to award badges?"
And that's the whole idea:
Your compliance audit trail IS your gamification event source. Same table. Same data. Two completely different products.
[ul][li]User completes an assessment → audit log captures it → SOC2 evidence ✅ → achievement unlocked 🏆[li]User assigns an application owner → audit log captures it → access control evidence ✅ → badge earned 🏆[li]User resolves a data quality flag → audit log captures it → monitoring evidence ✅ → streak continues 🏆
