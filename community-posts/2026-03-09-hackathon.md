What's up gang. I participated in a Hackathon this weekend. Mostly because I wanted to see what the whole thing is about. I learned a lot and it was fun. My idea was a gamified finances app \(not entirely there yet but I did like a prototype for the entry\). Granted, I never try to build games or none of that, I'm more focused on what my business is about, but I thought I needed something lightweight just to participate. Anyway, here are some screenshots of the app, it's called Payday Kingdom.

In simple terms: Your calendar is like a little landscape that has your bills walking around \(monsters\) and the day you get paid, your Hero \(the paycheck I guess, LOL\) comes in and slays the bills, and then you get XP. The more XP your Hero grows or is supposed to have new gear, and the land also levels with you. So after each bill is slayed you get the option to screenshot it and share it online for bragging rights. I though it was whimsical and a silly way to waste weekend tokens. I ended up learning quite a bit on how to operate Claude + Claude Code, OpenClaw + Codex, ChatGPT + Codex.

The Claude Code version ended up being more robust and stable, with less errors.
The BernieClaw version was pretty damn near the quality of the Claude Code one,
The Codex one ended up having the best looking UI overall but when I told it to do some changes to the game it ended breaking some stuff.

Anyway, it was a lot of fun and right now I am full time using my OpenClaw coding stuff for me while I work on other projects.
