Welcome, Agent Architects

So stoked to have you all here!

If you are curious about building AI-native businesses using tools like Claude Code, Codex, other command-line agents, multi-agent orchestration, and automation workflows, this is the community for you. Whether you're just getting started, coding up your own agents, or wiring workflows together, you're in the right place.

Before you dive in, go explore our chat forum tabs in the community and respond to at least two posts that interest you! When people like your posts, you gain points on the leaderboard to unlock special resources from the community. The top of the 30 day leaderboard gets a free 1 hour 1:1 call with me every month to focus on their projects, so post and comment as much as you can!

INTRODUCTIONS:

To really plug into this community and get to know our talented members, go to THE NEW Introductions tab, make a post, and say hi to our other Architects!

COURSES:

Claude Code Fundamentals: If you're newer to tools like Claude Code, start out with the Claude Code Fundamentals video course in the classroom section, and then check out the accompanying resource courses for skills, slash commands, subagents, and some of my own custom tools for scraping X and other cool applications after you've got the fundamentals.

CortextOS: Use Claude Code, Codex + Crons + Telegram to create the ultimate agent operating system. Agents receive and send messages via Telegram, have autonomous heartbeats, and can schedule workflows for you. Run experiments on each other, chat with each other behind the scenes, and help you automate business processes, all with a full customizable dashboard.

Fully Autonomous Development: If you're looking to create agent orchestration systems to fully autonomously create production ready software from a simple idea, check out the Fully Autonomous Development Framework course.

Tools & Skills: A list of skills and tools that I personally create and curate on a daily basis based off of daily AI news and releases to keep myself and the community at the cutting edge of AI tech. If you came from one of my posts about a custom skill or tool, you will find them in here.
