Hey everyone! My name is Sahil.

I’m currently on the path to becoming a doctor, but I’ve never really believed that becoming a physician should be the ceiling of my impact.
I’m fascinated by the intersection of medicine, biotech, and AI, and I feel like we’re entering a period where automation and intelligent systems will fundamentally reshape how healthcare \(and a lot of other industries\) work.

My background is a bit interdisciplinary — I studied biology, led a research team to study the interactions of the concentrations of Myosin in small vs large muscle fibers, worked as an EMT, and obtained my Masters in Business Analytics.
The unique blend of science, healthcare, and data analysis has truly opened my scope to how intelligent systems can scale solutions to complex real-world problems.

I’ve also started an official 501\(c\)3 nonprofit organization coined ForTheForestInc, focused on environmental restoration and community impact. This gave me a lot of perspective on how systems-level thinking is needed to tackle big challenges.

Recently I’ve been diving deep into agentic AI, automation workflows and how I can use ai to leverage autonomous development, because I feel like the people who understand these systems early will be able to build incredibly powerful tools.

I’m here to learn from people pushing the edge, exchange ideas, and see what kinds of systems people are building.

Excited to connect with everyone here!
