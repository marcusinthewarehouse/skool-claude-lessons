Months working to get all 7 of my crew on board the AI train, my last hold out was "the smart guy" - a talented developer trapped in an information echo-chamber of fellow AI haters. "AI is dumb." "It can't really do anything" or my favorite, "Vibecoding Schmibecoding". But last week, I demonstrated a new client details tool that I had been requesting since Moses. "Cool!" "Amazing!" said my crew. "It's not bad", said my holdout, "but who built it?" I yawned and explained "It's just a little something I threw together over the weekend. Claude helped a little." As I have difficulty doing literally anything, this was surreal. Now he is gulping our kool-aid by the bucket and driving everyone nuts with discovery enthusiasm. Nothing inspires like proof of concept. This was a big win for me.
