I know everyone is coming to the same conclusion here I'm just typing it out as I wait for my phone to get repaired here at the luxe nest that is the Northside Apple Store. I've been concentrated on documenting ideas \(destinations\) and the workflows that get me there \(roads\)... less focused on the particular plugins/methods/optimizations because in a world where things change constantly performance-maxxing becomes a road to doing nothing.

I also feel that nothing I'm saying or thinking is particularly original. Anyone who is maxxing out their max plans are finding the same answer: you gotta get shit done no matter what... even if the setup isn't fully optimized yet.

In a nutshell, I'm spending my BRAIN TOKENS on what it is I want to do and be... not as much how the hell I'll do it...because the latter constantly changes and it can be dizzying.
