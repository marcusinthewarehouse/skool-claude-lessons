# The Setup

What started as Paul asking Claude to roast him turned into something else—a moment of clarity about what he’s actually building and why.
-----
## The Initial Challenge
Claude questioned whether building enterprise-grade automation for 103 doors was the highest-leverage use of a CEO’s time. The architecture looked like overkill: 7+ integrations \(GHL, LeadSimple, DocuSign, Claude AI, RentVine, Gmail, Zillow, QuickBooks\), webhook chains, AI-powered PDF parsing—all for a company onboarding maybe 2-3 owners per month.

The question posed: *“Is this the highest-leverage use of your time as CEO?”*

-----

## The Reframe

Paul clarified: He’s not building for 103 doors. He’s building for 400-500 \(first milestone\) and 1,500 \(10-year goal\).

At 1,500 doors with normal turnover:

- 100-150 new owner onboardings per year
- Thousands of utility bills monthly
- Hundreds of lease renewals annually

The automation foundation suddenly made sense at that scale.

-----

## The Reveal

Then Paul dropped the real context:

**0 → 104 doors in 24 months. Started as a solo operator in January 2024.**
