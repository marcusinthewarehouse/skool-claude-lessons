Just dropped social-media-insights skill on GitHub.

The purpose of this skill is to give it to one of your agents that you can message via discord or telegram, so that when you're doom scrolling AItok or AI reels and find a good one, you can just send the link to your agent directly through the social media apps share button, it will scrape it and get all the info, and then think about how it can use that info to upgrade itself and upgrade your AI workflow.

It's a full 17-step Apify pipeline that scrapes TikTok, Instagram, and X posts, analyzes what's working, and generates agent recommendations based on what your audience is actually working on. Production ready. [https://github.com/grandamenium/social-media-insights](https://github.com/grandamenium/social-media-insights)
