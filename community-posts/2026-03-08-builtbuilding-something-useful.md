What I built with Claude Code this week: A private coaching intelligence dashboard that runs locally on my computer. Connected directly to my Fathom call recording account, pulled all 200+ coaching calls, and automatically matched each call to the right client using their email, name, and voice patterns from the transcript.

What it shows me right now:
[ul][li]All 9 clients in one view with status indicators \(how many days since last call\)[li]Click any client → full call history, every session summarized[li]Key takeaways and next steps from every call, fully expanded[li]Action items aggregated across all calls — separated by what the client owes vs. what I owe[li]Direct link to open any call in Fathom

Where it's going:
[ul][li]Program progress bars \(week they're in, how far through their program\)[li]Battleplan reference panel — what protocols I've assigned each client[li]Claude AI reading the actual transcripts to surface tendencies, language patterns, and what's really going on beneath the surface[li]Claude AI producing pre-call briefs to maximize efficiency on calls and actually focus on solving client roadblocks \(not catching up and trying to remember where we are / what we're doing\).[li]Post-call action item export — clean list ready to send the client immediately[li]Check-in data integration — cross-referenced against their goals & progress over time[li]Plus more mwahahah

Built entirely with Claude Code, zero prior coding experience. The whole thing cost a few hours of conversation.
