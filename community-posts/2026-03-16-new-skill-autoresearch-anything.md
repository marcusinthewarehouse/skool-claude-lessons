Apply Karpathy's autoresearch pattern to ANY business metric with an objectively verifiable success metric. This skill sets up an autonomous experimentation pipeline that modifies, measures, keeps or discards, and compounds improvements while you sleep.

What it includes:
[ul][li]Structured Q&A to scope your pipeline \(metric, platform, constraints, measurement window\)[li]- Deep domain research before the first experiment[li]- Karpathy-style [program.md](http://program.md) with the full experiment loop[li]- Three immutable knowledge files + compounding [resource.md](http://resource.md) for learnings[li]- Connector stubs that your Claude builds WITH you during setup[li]- Persistent execution via launchd + tmux or GitHub Actions
Works for cold email reply rates, landing page conversions, ad CTR, newsletter open rates, pricing page optimization, and anything else you can measure with an API.

Clone into your skills directory and run /autoresearch-anything to start the guided setup.[https://github.com/grandamenium/autoresearch-anything](https://github.com/grandamenium/autoresearch-anything)
