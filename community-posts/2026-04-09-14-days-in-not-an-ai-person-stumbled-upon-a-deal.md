I work with an AI agency group. Marketing side, not tech.

14 days ago I watched a YouTube video and started using Claude Code.

I didn't even know it was a thing.

This is what I found.

I spent a weekend at my wife's aunt's house.

She owns a small hotel. 48 rooms. 600 guests a month.

600 people who just got to a city they don't know. Hungry. Clueless about where to go.

"Every day people call the front desk asking for tips on anything you can imagine" she said.

Huh. I bet the restaurant next door would've paid good money for a QR code in every room.

Turns out nobody ever asked.

So I whip up my laptop. Claude Code and I build a "concierge" page. She gives me the credentials, we upload it.

She's not in the room business. She's in the people's distribution business.

Just like I'm not in the AI business. I'm in the 'Asset + Distribution' business \(that's a thing, I swear\)

I'm building her the full concierge. Free.

Because my asset gives me access to her flow of people.

Now I can rent space to restaurants, bars, night clubs, laundromats. Anything that needs 600 warm bodies a month.

One small hotel easily = 2-3k a month, fully automated.

Also... How hard is it to scale? My city alone has 58 other hotels in the same exact category.

I'm an AI newbie. Go light on me.

But, in the end, it's a tool.

The REAL asset - is gaining ACO over assets and flows.

ACO = Access / Control / Ownership

Auntie is happy... guests are happy... I'm happy.

And I just KNOW you have a few 'hotels' right in front of your face. I know I miss a few every single day.

\(Hey, if you use my idea, I'm happy with 10% royalties :\)
