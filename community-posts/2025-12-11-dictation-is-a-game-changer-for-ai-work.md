Quick tip for anyone still typing everything out when talking to AI: look into Whispering.

There are several paid dictation services, but the one I'm using is free, open source, runs locally, and works great. No monthly subscription and worth the few extra moments of setup time which Opus 4.5 sliced right through. I'm using it with Claude Code which runs inside a terminal for me in VS Code. Just wanted to share this as it's been a boon for me. Once it's set up, you just hit a hotkey and talk. Way faster than typing and less fatiguing for me.
