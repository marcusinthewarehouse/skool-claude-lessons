Most people are drowning in contacts, follow-ups, and context that lives across their inbox, calendar, and notes app, never connected, never queryable.

This template fixes that.

The Agentic CRM Assistant is a new cortextOS community template that gives you a persistent personal CRM agent: one that remembers people, tracks your relationships, preps you for meetings, triages your inbox, and surfaces follow-ups before they fall through the cracks.

It is built for anyone from solo operators to executives. Bring your own tools through connectors, MCP, CLI tools, browser automation, or env vars.

What you get on first run:
- A full interactive setup that walks you through identity, tools, CRM policy, privacy, schedule, and approval rules
- Structured local CRM: contacts, companies, interactions, follow-up queue, relationship health scores

Built-in recurring workflows:
- Morning/evening calendar review
- Inbox triage with draft-and-hold \(never sends without your go\)
- Pending-items digest
- Meeting notes processing
- Weekly relationship review
- Follow-up tracking with auto-surface

CRM skills included:
- Contact management and upsert
- Meeting prep \(pulls context on everyone in the room\)
- Email triage with draft queue
- Calendar management
- Relationship health review

Safety gates on everything that matters: external messages, bookings, purchases, deletions, financial actions, and material changes all require your approval before anything happens.

How to install:
1. Ask your cortextOS orchestrator to spin up the agentic-crm-assistant template: just say "spin up a new agent using the agentic-crm-assistant template"
2. Once your new bot is live on Telegram, open that chat and run /setup to complete onboarding

That is it. Two steps and you have a running CRM agent.
