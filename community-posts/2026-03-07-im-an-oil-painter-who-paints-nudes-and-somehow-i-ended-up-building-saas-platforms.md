Hi everyone!
My name is Cassidy and I am located in Los Angeles, CA.

I’m a fine artist… an oil painter. That’s the world I come from and the business I built first. I paint large pieces, a lot of figurative work, and I’m especially known for painting sensual fine art nudes. The human form has always fascinated me… emotion, power, vulnerability, identity… all of it. A lot of my work explores those themes.

Over time my work started intersecting with the luxury watch world and collector culture. I began painting watches… integrating them into my work… and my paintings ended up resonating with people who love watches, craftsmanship, design, rarity… that whole ecosystem.

So art is the foundation of everything I do.

Alongside that, I also coach artists. I work with other creatives on the business side of their careers… marketing their work, pricing, positioning, communicating with collectors, building sustainable art businesses. Helping artists understand the market and their own value is something I care a lot about.

A big part of that has made me really interested in building tools for artists too. Most artists want to stay in their creative space… painting, sculpting, creating… not getting pulled into spreadsheets, marketing systems, sales pipelines, email lists, and all the business infrastructure that ends up being required.

So one of the things I’m really excited about with AI is building systems that actually support artists in that world… tools that help with marketing, social media, list building, communicating with collectors, structuring offers, managing sales… all the things that normally drain creative energy.

Over the last five or six years I went deep down the rabbit hole of how artists actually price their work… which eventually led me to build a SaaS platform \(Thanks Lovable!\) that helps artists understand all the variables behind pricing and teaches them how to price their work ethically and sustainably… because most artists are thinking about survival… not long-term business.
