So in the spirit of getting to know each other beyond our AI era, I’m starting an IMPORTANT thread.

Drop your pet coworkers below. 🐾

I’ll go first.

Meet Elwood. 45 pounds. Pure. Beef. 🐷

He is a king-sized pug who takes up roughly 80% of my desk chair and 100% of my heart. We adopted him while living our best digital nomad life wandering the US, they told us he was 4 years old and a very reasonable 20 lbs.

Friends… he was 4 MONTHS. 😂

We have since accepted our fate. He is now 2.5 years old, has never once moved off the couch voluntarily, and audibly snores through every single Zoom call. He contributes nothing to my productivity and everything to my mental health.

Your turn. Show me your WFH coworkers…the fluffy ones, the scaly ones, the ones who walk across your keyboard at the worst possible moment. Let’s see ’em! 👇
