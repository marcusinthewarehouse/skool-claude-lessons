For entrepreneurs who struggle to effectively communicate the unique value of their ideas, products, or changes, this Value Proposition Recipe provides a clear, structured method to articulate and deliver key benefits by breaking down the message into essential, impactful components... better yet, use it to create a simple little data table or md file that your AI \(.claude\) can reference as context for it's 'fall back purpose'.

As opposed to generic messaging strategies or complex marketing jargon, this Value Proposition Recipe offers a simple, replicable framework that ensures your audience instantly understands the unique value you bring to the table.

By using this proven recipe, you can ensure your ideas resonate more powerfully and persuasively with your target audience, leading to greater engagement and success because it "positions" your "value" relative to someone's NEEDS, how they are CURRENTLY being met, and how you are different from the competitors that currently meet those needs, including, status quo.

Try it and see how this straightforward approach can transform your messaging!

The Value Proposition Recipe \(ingredients are inside the "\[" and "\]"\)

For \[insert market segment or the stakeholders your thing delivers benefits to\] who \[insert market segment KEY NEED\], our \[Insert the thing or change you're 'selling'\] provides \[insert the KEY BENEFIT provided by your thing or change you're selling\] by \[explain HOW the thing or change you're selling delivers benefits\].

As opposed to/unlike \[insert status quo or leading alternative approach to meeting the market segment need\], our \[insert the thing or change you're 'selling'\] \[insert the differentiator that distinguishes why the thing or change you're selling is UNIQUE and CAN'T BE REPLICATED\].

Get it? If so, you've just experienced firsthand the effectiveness of the Value Proposition Recipe in action. Didn't get it? Have you ever watched a really cheezy Infomercial at 3AM stoned or drunk eating pizza? They use the value prop recipe explicitly and exclusively... Now, apply it to your own ideas, products, or changes and watch the impact! Impact = it establishes the 'mental velcro' that is relevant to your AI... wanna test it? start by not building a value prop... go to 5 people and "pitch your idea".... ask them "did you understand it?" and "would you be interested in knowing more?"... then apply the recipe, and try it again... it will likely increase audience awareness, understanding, and desire...
