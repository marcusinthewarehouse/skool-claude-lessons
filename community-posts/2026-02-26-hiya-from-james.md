Me:

Now, because of AI, software, social media, and all of you, I get to spend my time investigating, experimenting on, and trying to understand the new brains of these artificial neural networks we call LLMs.

I am also just about to launch two SaaS products that I've been working on with my business partner, and am packing to move down to him in Florida next week.

The Community:

Currently our courses focus on the fundamentals of Claude Code, autonomous development systems with Claude Code, and using agents like Claude Code and OpenClaw to organize our lives to be more productive and AI native.
