Working on the go-to-market strategy for Dane IQ and wanted to share our approach and get some feedback.

We are planning to do direct sales only cold email, SMS, and phone calls. No paid advertising, some retargeting. We are going directly after property management companies that would benefit from our platform, and we are researching each one before reaching out so every message is relevant and personal. We understand the legal requirements around cold outreach \(CAN-SPAM, TCPA\) and are building those into the process from the start.

Would love for anyone here to look over the plan and share feedback, opinions, or ideas. If you have experience in B2B SaaS sales, direct outreach, or property management, your perspective would be especially valuable.
