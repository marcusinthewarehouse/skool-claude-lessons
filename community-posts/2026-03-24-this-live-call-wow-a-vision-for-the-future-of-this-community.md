Let me firstly thank every one of you that joined the live today. One of the best we've ever had, because my mind is absolutely SPINNING with ideas.

For anyone who couldn't attend and is interested in becoming more invested in the community, especially for the long haul, I strongly encourage you to watch the whole video.

We talked almost the entire call about where the community is headed and landed on a pretty clear conclusion:

Members need and want to be able to collaborate, communicate, and work together more seamlessly. On top of that, each member of this community has their own domain expertise that can benefit the entire community if there's an easy way to share it.

This idea will underly the future of this community. This will be facilitated by a few things:

1\) Projects that I release in this community will from now on be fully open to contributions from any community member. No matter your experience level or expertise, you are all encouraged to modify, specify, and improve all of the projects I release and your changes will be shared with the rest of the community with your permission. Whether through repo PRs, posts, or other.

2\) I'm actively seeking out an alternative to Skool to organize us as a community. We need the ability for members to organize their own calls, freely create group chats and DM each other, and domain channels and classrooms for different real world applications where you can find individuals and business owners using AI to build the same systems you want to build.

3\) I will be reorganizing the classroom to be simpler, and more focused on teaching you how to use these tools to achieve outcomes in the real world in your lives and businesses, focused on giving you the shortest path to achieving your goals.

This post is the beginning of this new shift. Treat the comments section below this post like the first baby step of the new version of this community.

Post your name, what you do for work or business, how you're using AI to help you, and most importantly, what expert domain do you command? What can you offer to other members in exchange for their expertise? Expert is a loose term. ANY knowledge you have is valuable. List any contact details you're comfortable sharing \(will be kept private in this community\) and organize yourselves! Connect with those people that interest you and who share your ideas.
