This is the space for sharing and tracking your ongoing projects.

The General and Casual Chat sections work great for quick questions and conversations, but they can get noisy when you're trying to follow along with someone's build journey or need sustained help on a specific project.

This section is different. Create a post for your project and use it as a living thread where you:

- Share what you're working on and your goals
- Post updates as you make progress
- Ask for help when you hit blockers
- Get feedback from the community
- Document what worked \(and what didn't\)

Think of it like a dev log that the whole community can follow and contribute to.

If you're building something with Claude Code, agents, automation, or anything AI-native - start a thread here. The community can jump in with suggestions, resources, and support as you go.
