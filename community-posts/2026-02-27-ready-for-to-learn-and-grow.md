My name is David Hunter, and I live in Chattanooga, Tennessee. My wife and I own a property management company. I’m also a licensed general contractor, and for the last three years I’ve basically been a glorified maintenance director. Honestly, I hate it.

Last September, I got determined to build an AI-based maintenance management system called Dane IQ, named after our three Great Danes. The goal was simple: create something that could handle all the tasks my ADD brain struggled to consistently follow through on.

I started building the backend in Replit and used Lovable for the frontend. After a while, I realized the backend in Replit had become pretty messy. About four weeks ago, I connected GitHub Copilot with Claude Code and started digging into the codebase to clean it up and really understand what I had built.

That process made me realize there’s a lot more I don’t know than I thought. So to accelerate my growth, I decided to join and surround myself with like-minded individuals who are building and learning at a high level
