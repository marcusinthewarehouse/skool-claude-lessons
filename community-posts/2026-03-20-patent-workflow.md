I have to share this. So ive been working on this for a while now. When using AI to help draft patents. I draft my ideas and build my spec then my claims. If you dont know how it works its like this. You can file a provisional patent. That locks in your priority invention date. If you do not have your name on more than 5 awarded patents you can file as a micro entity. The filing fee is $65 dollars. Then you have 12 months to convert that to a non-provisional patent. Which is much more costly and i recommend using an attorney. But ive used a 6 agent analysis that has helped me draft the most solid provisionals. Have claude code set up 6 agents to analyze the patent from the POV of a patent examiner \(which is the uspto employee that awards or declines\), a patent attorney, a patent law judge, a competitor or hacker that would find exploits, an expert in the field of invention, and a regulatory expert. With this you can confidently self file your provisional giving you patent pending status. And buying you time to raise revenue to pay for expert conversions later. After office actions and prior art searches
plan on paying around $10-$15k+ for final conversion with an attorney. A lot of attorneys prefere to draft your provisional too. They can advise on what to leave out and what to include. But you cant change the spec after you file a provisional. You can only add claims that are supported by the spec. So if your confident in your spec you can self file. Now you should do your research for what works best for your application and obviously this is not legal advice. But ive been confident in filing using multiple 6+ agent analysises. Hope this helps.
