I just wanted to take the time to circle back on last Monday's group call. I know a lot of people in the community are looking for ways to monetize this Cortex OS, looking for ways to create and do things. Some people are trying to use this to create and build awesome apps and do that, which, more power to you. I think it's fucking phenomenal.

For the rest of you guys that are like me and have no idea what's going on when it comes to the idea of building your own SAAS, But I am thinking about how to use this for something. I really think there's a huge opportunity to use this as a system to go to other businesses and implement AI for small businesses in the local community, a certain niche, showing them how to take AI agents and run their business or the software they're currently using.

One of the things I love about this positioning is you're not worried about the market shifts. You're not worried about the larger models, other people coming in and creating new things. There will always be small business owners who need someone to help them implement. There will always be people wanting to understand where the market is going.

As frontier models create agents, do things that Cortex OS can do, you can shift and now have understanding how to set them up for them. As free models come up, you'd be able to say, "Hey, you can use a Cortex OS system on your own server." You have the ability to shift with the market while maintaining your ability in personal brand, business brand, and the community or niche that you have selected.

That's why I say sell shovels during the AI Gold Rush.
