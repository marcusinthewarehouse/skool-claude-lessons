This weekend, I installed/configured an openbrain that lets me keep all my own intelligence centralized, and 'separate from' all the AI platforms I use. No matter what AI solution I chat with, it's all threaded through a database that doesn't care about which AI I'm using. my knowledge/intel is MINE and no matter what the AI solutions do or don't do, I will always have it, and do what I want with it. I'm a data guy, and my client solutions are all rooted in the principle "if you don't have a data model you don't have shit"... I can help you get your data model built... which is the foundation of your company/idea... it's the ground truth. Where I need help is automating that process... I have been slowly automating the data pipeline \(creation of the model\) by building data management skills. once you have this data model created THEN your agents can start doing shit, using a ground truth, and that's where the fun actually begins... that's where I 'overlay' my own management consulting 'skills'... the secret sauce that turns that data model intel into actionable decisions. this is why I want to build my solution, which is basically a business owner GPS that works like Google Maps.
