I was on the phone with a small flooring company to discuss repair work needed.

We know each other a bit and started to chat about ai and the types of software it can create. He really impressed me with all the ways he tried to automate his company.

One thing that stuck out in the conversation, every time I thought we were getting to something he wanted to automate, he’d bring up an app that he either uses or tried. Lots of things exist in the world already, before spending time and tokens maybe do a quick search and see if the product is out there. 😀
