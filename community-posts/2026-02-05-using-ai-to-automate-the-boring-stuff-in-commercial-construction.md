## Using AI to Automate the Boring Stuff in Commercial Construction

I’m a commercial construction PM — 18 years in the game, projects up to $500M. Tilt-wall, hospitality, healthcare. For the last several months I’ve been using AI \(Claude + Claude Code\) to automate the repetitive process work that eats up my day.

Here’s what’s actually working:

**Scope of Work generation** — I feed in project specs and trade requirements, and AI drafts a full CSI-formatted subcontractor scope with inclusions, exclusions, and general conditions. What took 2-3 hours now takes 20 minutes of review.

**Spec document querying** — On my current $20M+ project, instead of digging through thousands of pages of specs to find a fire-rating requirement or concrete mix design, I query the documents conversationally and get the answer with the section reference in seconds.

**Pay app reconciliation** — Comparing a sub’s billing against cost projections and the SOV used to be a full afternoon of spreadsheet work. Now I have tools that flag the variances and I just review the exceptions.

**Training docs and process systems** — Built comprehensive Procore training materials, QC systems, and reporting templates in a fraction of the time it would have taken manually.

The key insight: AI doesn’t replace the PM. It gives you back the hours you’re burning on admin so you can spend more time in the field, on the phone with subs, and actually managing the project.

You don’t need to be a developer to start. I wasn’t. Start with the work that’s repetitive and structured — scopes, RFIs, meeting minutes, pay app reviews — and build from there.
