Okay… I just have to share this because I’m honestly so freaking excited right now.

I’ve been working through the M2C1 framework \(I mean, technically it is a skill\) and the last couple days have felt like my brain just unlocked a new level.

It took me a little bit to wrap my head around Claude Code because I’m still pretty new to this level of building… but once things started clicking it was like… oh my god… this is actually possible.

Right now I’m building out a SaaS platform that involves a pretty massive backend database, market data, and a lot of moving parts… and watching it slowly start to take shape has been kind of surreal.

One part of the process that really surprised me happened after I had already given it a pretty big brain dump of what I wanted to build.

It came back with something like 50 questions.

At first I was like… okay wow… this is a lot lol.

But they were actually really straightforward questions. It was basically trying to understand the logic of the system… how things should behave… how users interact with it… what the structure should look like… things like that.

Honestly it ended up being incredibly helpful because it forced me to think through the architecture of what I’m building in a way I probably wouldn’t have done on my own yet.

And if a question went a little above my head… I just threw it into GPT and had it help me understand what it was asking so I could answer properly.

So the whole process felt a lot less intimidating than it sounds.

Now I’m in the middle of working through Claude Code… structuring things… figuring out how the pieces fit together… and watching the system slowly start to come alive.

My brain is honestly kind of exploding right now… in the best way.
This stuff is so freaking cool.
